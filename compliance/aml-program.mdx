LoanOrigination.ca supports your anti-money laundering program with risk rating, ongoing monitoring, politically exposed person (PEP) and sanctions screening, suspicious transaction workflows, large cash transaction handling, and compliance officer dashboards. These tools help you meet current Canadian regulatory obligations and maintain consistent record keeping across your portfolio.

<Note>
  AML monitoring rules are maintained in line with current Canadian regulatory obligations.
</Note>

## Set up client risk ratings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the compliance profile">
    From the borrower or contact record, open the **AML / Compliance** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a risk level">
    Select a risk rating based on your internal policy. Common ratings include low, medium, and high, and are configurable per lender.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document the rationale">
    Enter the factors that informed the rating, such as geography, business sector, transaction patterns, or ownership structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set review frequency">
    Define how often the risk rating and monitoring results must be reviewed based on the assigned level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run PEP and sanctions screening

The platform screens borrowers, guarantors, and beneficial owners against PEP and sanctions lists as part of the identity verification and ongoing monitoring workflows.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger screening">
    Screening runs automatically during identity verification and at scheduled intervals. You can also initiate a manual check from the **Compliance** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matches">
    Open the screening result to review potential matches, including match confidence and list source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record resolution">
    Confirm or clear the match, attach supporting rationale, and save the outcome to the compliance file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Escalate suspicious transactions

When a transaction or pattern of activity raises concern, use the built-in escalation workflow to document, route, and report the issue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Flag the activity">
    From the transaction record or monitoring alert, select **Flag as Suspicious** and describe the observed behaviour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route for review">
    The alert is routed to the assigned compliance officer or queue based on your configured escalation path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document the decision">
    Record the review outcome, including whether the activity was cleared, escalated further, or reported externally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handle large cash transactions

For cash transactions that meet or exceed the reporting threshold, the system prompts for required details at the point of entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the transaction">
    Enter the payment or deposit in the servicing module. If the amount meets the threshold, a **Large Cash Transaction** form appears automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete required fields">
    Fill in the payer details, source of funds, and transaction purpose as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for compliance review">
    Save the record. It is added to the compliance queue and included in relevant regulatory submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use the compliance officer dashboard

The compliance dashboard gives you a central view of open alerts, overdue reviews, pending verifications, and recent screening results. Use it to prioritise daily compliance tasks, monitor queue workload, and prepare for audit or regulatory review.

<Tip>
  All AML actions, from risk rating changes to screening results and escalation decisions, are captured in the [audit trail](/compliance/audit-trail) with an immutable timestamp and user attribution.
</Tip>

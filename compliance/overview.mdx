The Compliance and Reporting layer in LoanOrigination.ca brings together identity verification, anti-money laundering controls, funder reporting, bilingual delivery, analytics, and an immutable audit trail into a single system. This layer is designed to reduce manual regulatory work, standardise reporting, and help lenders meet Canadian obligations with confidence.

Use this layer to verify borrower identity, monitor AML risk, generate impact reports for funders, produce operational and regulatory reports, serve borrowers in English and French, and maintain a complete record of every system action for audit and regulatory review.

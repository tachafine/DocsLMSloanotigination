---

LoanOrigination.ca delivers a complete English and French interface to support Canadian community lenders operating in bilingual markets. Every user can switch their preferred language independently, and the platform supports bilingual notification templates, document templates, Quebec-specific form variants, and borrower-facing materials in both languages.

## Change your language preference

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile settings">
    Click your name or avatar in the top navigation bar and select **Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a language">
    Choose **English** or **French** from the **Language** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and confirm">
    Click **Save**. The interface reloads in your selected language immediately. All menus, labels, and system text display in the chosen language.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Language selection is per user. One staff member can work in English while another works in French, with no impact on shared records or data.
</Note>

## Manage bilingual notification templates

Notification templates for borrowers and staff can be maintained in both English and French. When a notification is triggered, the platform sends the version matching the recipient's language preference.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open template management">
    Navigate to **Administration > Templates > Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit or create a template">
    Select an existing template or click **New Template**. You will see tabs for **English** and **French** content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter both language versions">
    Fill in the subject line and body text for each language. Use the same merge fields in both versions so dynamic data populates correctly regardless of language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and set active">
    Save the template and mark it active. The platform automatically selects the correct language version at send time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work with bilingual document templates

Document templates, such as loan agreements, commitment letters, and disclosure statements, can also be maintained in English and French. When generating a document for a borrower, select the language version or let the system default to the borrower's profile language.

## Use Quebec-specific form variants

For lenders operating in Quebec, the platform supports form variants that reflect Quebec-specific regulatory and terminology requirements. These variants are configured during implementation and can be assigned to borrowers based on region or program.

## Deliver bilingual borrower-facing materials

Borrowers using the web portal and mobile application see the interface in their selected language. All borrower-facing text, notifications, status updates, and help content are rendered in English or French based on the borrower's profile setting.

<Tip>
  Keep both language versions of templates current. When updating a template in one language, review the other version to ensure consistency in terms, obligations, and formatting.
</Tip>

LoanOrigination.ca provides identity verification workflows aligned to Canadian requirements. You can verify individual and business borrowers using document verification, dual process, and credit file methods, with automatic tracking of beneficial ownership and re-verification triggers.

## Verify an individual borrower

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the borrower profile">
    Navigate to the contact or application record and open the **Identity Verification** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a verification method">
    Choose **Document Verification**, **Dual Process**, or **Credit File** based on your lender policy and the information available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit or review evidence">
    For document verification, upload or review the identity document. For dual process, enter the two independent information sources. For credit file, confirm the credit bureau match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm verification status">
    Record the outcome, attach any supporting notes, and set a re-verification date if required by policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify beneficial ownership for business borrowers

When a business applies for a loan, identify and verify each beneficial owner through the **Business Details** section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add business entity details">
    Enter the business name, registration number, and structure in the borrower profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare beneficial owners">
    List individuals who own or control 25 percent or more of the business, or who exercise significant control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify each owner">
    Run the same verification methods (document, dual process, or credit file) for each listed owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record and save">
    Save the ownership structure and verification outcomes to the compliance file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage re-verification triggers

The system supports re-verification triggers based on configurable rules. Common triggers include:

- Change in borrower contact or address information
- Significant change in loan terms or structure
- Periodic policy-driven review cycle
- Flag raised by AML monitoring or sanctions screening

When a trigger is activated, the borrower or business profile displays a **Re-verification Required** status. Complete the verification steps again and record the new outcome before proceeding with the application or disbursement.

<Tip>
  Verification outcomes are logged automatically to the [audit trail](/compliance/audit-trail), including the method used, the user who completed the check, and the timestamp.
</Tip>

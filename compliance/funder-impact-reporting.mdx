LoanOrigination.ca automates the collection of impact data needed for quarterly and annual funder reports. Instead of assembling spreadsheets manually, you can configure the platform to gather jobs created and maintained, demographic fields (where permitted), sector, region, and investment outcomes directly from loan records and borrower profiles, then generate reports in the formats your funders require.

## Configure impact data collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define required fields">
    Work with your implementation team to map the impact fields your funders require to fields in LoanOrigination.ca. Common fields include jobs created, jobs maintained, sector, region, and demographic categories where collection is permitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set collection points">
    Configure where each field is captured. For example, jobs data may be collected at application and updated at annual review. Demographic fields may be collected during intake if permitted by your policies and funder agreements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable validation rules">
    Add mandatory field checks and range validations so incomplete or inconsistent data is flagged before submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generate a funder impact report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report library">
    Navigate to **Compliance > Reports > Funder Impact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the reporting period">
    Choose the quarter or year, and select the funder format required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and adjust">
    Preview the aggregated data, check for flagged records, and enter any manual adjustments with documented rationale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or submit">
    Export the report in the required format, or submit it through the configured channel if your implementation includes direct funder integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule recurring reports

You can schedule impact reports to run automatically at the end of each quarter or fiscal year. Scheduled reports are generated, saved to the document repository, and distributed to the configured recipients by email. You can also set reminders for review and approval before the scheduled send date.

<Warning>
  Demographic fields are collected only where permitted by your policies and funder agreements. Do not enable demographic collection without confirming compliance with your privacy and consent framework.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Impact data is drawn from live loan and borrower records, so keeping application and annual review data current improves report accuracy and reduces manual correction at quarter end.
</Tip>

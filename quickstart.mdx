This quickstart walks a new lender administrator through the first configuration of LoanOrigination.ca and the end-to-end path of a single loan, from intake to disbursement.

## Prerequisites

<Note>
  You need an Administrator account, your organization's branding assets, and any active agreements with external providers (credit bureau, identity verification) you plan to enable.
</Note>

## Set up your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in as Administrator">
    Sign in to your production environment using the credentials provided at implementation kickoff. Confirm your workspace name, logo, and colour palette in **Settings > Branding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    In **Settings > Users**, invite staff and assign standard roles (Administrator, Manager, Officer, Processor, Read only) or a configured custom role. See [Roles and access](/get-started/roles-and-access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your first loan program">
    In **Configuration > Loan Programs**, create a loan product with its purpose, amount range, interest rate structure, term, payment frequency, fees, and eligibility rules. See [Loan programs](/origination/loan-programs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable integrations">
    In **Settings > Integrations**, connect the Canadian credit bureau, identity verification service, and notification providers you have agreements with. See [Integrations](/admin/integrations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Originate your first loan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive an application">
    A borrower submits an application through the web portal or mobile app. It appears in your officer queue with all uploaded documents attached. See [Application intake](/origination/application-intake).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Underwrite and decide">
    Review the file, pull credit, run the rules engine, and record a decision with rationale. Applications below the instant lending threshold auto-approve if they satisfy your rule set. See [Credit decisioning](/origination/credit-decisioning).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to committee">
    For files that require committee review, publish the package, notify members, and open the vote window. Decisions update loan status directly. See [Committee overview](/committee/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disburse funds">
    Confirm conditions precedent, approve the disbursement, and generate payment instructions. The amortisation schedule is created automatically. See [Disbursement](/servicing/disbursement).
  </Step>
</Steps>

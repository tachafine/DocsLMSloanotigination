LoanOrigination.ca portfolio analytics give you a real-time view of your lending book so you can monitor performance, manage risk, and report to stakeholders. This page covers the standard dashboards available in the Servicing area and how to configure and export reports for your board and funders.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Balance & Composition" icon="chart-pie">
    See outstanding principal and accrued interest sliced by loan program, sector, region, and borrower type. Use this view to understand concentration and diversification at a glance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Aging & Delinquency" icon="calendar-x">
    Track loans by days past due with configurable buckets. Identify trends in late payments and flag accounts that need follow-up before they move into default.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Yield & Performance" icon="trending-up">
    Measure portfolio yield, weighted average rate, and net income. Compare performance across programs and time periods to spot your most effective products.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Provisions & Reserves" icon="shield">
    Review required provisions and reserve allocations based on aging and risk ratings. Adjust assumptions and model the impact on your financial statements.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Funded Volume" icon="banknote">
    View disbursements and approvals over any date range. Filter by program, officer, or region to report on lending activity for a given quarter or funding cycle.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cash Flow Forecast" icon="calendar-check">
    Project incoming principal and interest payments over the next 12 to 36 months. Use this to manage liquidity and plan future funding rounds.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Configuring and exporting reports

Each dashboard can be filtered by date range, program, region, and officer. Once you have applied the filters you need, you can save the view for quick access later or export it for external distribution.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a dashboard">
    Navigate to **Servicing > Portfolio Analytics** and select the dashboard you want to view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the filter bar to narrow by date range, loan program, sector, region, or loan officer. The charts and tables update in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the view">
    Click **Save View** and give it a name, such as "Q3 Board Report" or "Aging by Officer." Saved views appear in your sidebar for one-click access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export** and choose PDF for presentations or CSV for spreadsheet analysis. Exports respect your current filters and date range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Schedule monthly exports to send automatically to your board or funders. Set the schedule from the **Exports** panel inside any saved view.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Cash flow forecasts use scheduled payment dates. Actual collections may differ based on borrower behavior and any deferrals or modifications applied.
</Note>

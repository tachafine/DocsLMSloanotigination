After a loan is funded, circumstances may require changes to the original terms. LoanOrigination.ca supports a full range of post-funding modifications, each governed by the approval workflow configured for your tenant. Every modification generates a new repayment schedule and preserves the previous terms in a permanent audit trail so you, your auditors, and your funders can reconstruct the full history of the account.

## Types of loan modifications

The platform supports the following modification categories:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Payment Deferral" icon="calendar-x">
    Postpone one or more scheduled payments. Interest may continue to accrue or be capitalized depending on program settings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Rate or Term Change" icon="percent">
    Adjust the interest rate or extend or shorten the remaining term. The system recalculates the schedule from the effective date.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Principal Restructuring" icon="scale">
    Reduce the outstanding principal, convert a portion to a separate note, or re-amortize the balance over a new term.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Guarantor Change" icon="user-plus">
    Add, remove, or replace a guarantor. The system triggers a new guarantor review workflow and updates security records.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Modification request workflow

All modifications follow a controlled request-and-approval process to prevent unauthorized changes to funded loans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the loan servicing record">
    Navigate to **Servicing** > **Loans** and select the account you want to modify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the modification type">
    Click **Request Modification** and select the category. The system presents the fields relevant to that change type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter new terms and justification">
    Complete the modification form. Provide the proposed new terms and a written justification for audit purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    The request routes to the approvers configured for your tenant. You can track status from the loan's modification history tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the modification">
    Once approved, click **Apply Modification**. The system generates a new repayment schedule and notifies the borrower.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The number and type of approvers required depend on the modification category and amount. Configure approval rules in **Settings** > **Servicing** > **Modification Policies**.
</Note>

## Audit trail and schedule history

Every modification is recorded in the loan's permanent history. The audit entry includes:

- The original and new terms
- The user who requested the change
- The approvers who authorized it
- The timestamp of application
- The justification text entered at submission

Borrowers can view their current schedule in the portal. Previous schedules are not visible to the borrower but remain accessible to servicing staff and auditors.

<Tip>
  Before requesting a modification, review the borrower's payment history and any active collection or case files. The **Account Summary** sidebar shows all open items at a glance.
</Tip>

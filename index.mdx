LoanOrigination.ca is a Canadian multi-tenant loan origination and loan management platform for community lenders. It covers the full loan lifecycle in a single auditable system: intake, underwriting, credit decisioning, committee review, approval, disbursement, servicing, repayment, collections, and closure.

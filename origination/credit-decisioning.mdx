LoanOrigination.ca credit decisioning pulls live bureau data from Equifax Canada and TransUnion Canada, evaluates your configured rules, and produces a recorded recommendation with full rationale. Officers review the recommendation, add overrides if needed, and the decision is permanently attached to the loan file.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the Scorecard">
    Go to **Origination > Decisioning > Scorecards**. Create or edit a scorecard. Add criteria such as debt service ratio thresholds, credit score bands, employment tenure minimums, and bankruptcy history flags. Assign points or pass/fail logic per criterion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Bureau Sources">
    In the scorecard settings, enable **Equifax Canada**, **TransUnion Canada**, or both. Map the data fields you want evaluated: Beacon score, total tradelines, delinquency counts, public records, and inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Decision Thresholds">
    Define the score or rule outcome that triggers each recommendation tier: Auto-Approve, Approve, Review, or Decline. Add mandatory override reasons for any tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Decision on an Application">
    Open a loan file and click **Run Decision**. The system fetches bureau data, evaluates rules, and displays the recommendation with a rationale panel showing which rules passed, failed, or were marginal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the Decision">
    The officer accepts the recommendation or selects an override with a documented reason. The final decision, rationale, and bureau snapshot are saved to the file and cannot be altered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>

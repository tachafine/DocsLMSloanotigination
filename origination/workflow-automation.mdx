LoanOrigination.ca workflow automation replaces manual handoffs with configurable approval chains that route applications to the right officers, escalate stalled files, and log every status change for audit. Managers see real-time pipeline views and identify bottlenecks before they breach SLAs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workflow Builder">
    From the **Origination** menu, select **Workflows** then click **New Workflow**. Name the workflow and choose the loan program it applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Stages and Assignees">
    Click **Add Stage** for each step (for example, Intake Review, Credit Analysis, Manager Approval). Assign individual officers, roles, or queues. Set whether a stage requires one approver or unanimous consensus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set SLA and Escalation Rules">
    For each stage, enter the SLA in business hours. Choose the escalation path: notify the assigned officer first, then their manager, then a designated backup. Add reminder intervals before escalation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Notifications">
    Select notification channels per event: new assignment, SLA warning, escalation, or final decision. Channels include in-app alerts, email, and SMS. Customize message templates in English and French.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Monitor">
    Toggle the workflow to **Active**. Use the **Pipeline Dashboard** to view files by stage, average time-in-stage, and files approaching SLA breach.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pipeline Visibility" icon="eye">

<Tip>
  Set a buffer reminder at 80 percent of SLA time so officers can act before escalation triggers.
</Tip>

Instant lending in LoanOrigination.ca lets you auto-approve and disburse qualifying applications without officer touch. You set a configurable threshold, product scope, and required KYC status; applications that satisfy every rule move straight to funding, while anything that fails routes to manual underwriting automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Instant Lending Settings">
    Navigate to **Origination > Decisioning > Instant Lending**. Toggle the feature on for your tenant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Amount and Product Scope">
    Enter the maximum loan amount eligible for instant approval. Select which loan programs or products are included. You can run instant lending for micro-loans while requiring manual review for commercial term loans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Required KYC Status">
    Choose the FINTRAC KYC/AML verification level an applicant must reach before instant lending activates. Options include identity verified, PEP/sanctions screened, and beneficial ownership confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Rule Pass Criteria">
    Select the scorecard and decision tier that qualifies for auto-approval. Typically this is the highest tier with zero marginal or failed rules. Require a minimum internal impact score if hybrid scoring is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Disbursement and Fallback">
    Choose the funding source and disbursement method for approved instant loans. Define the fallback queue where files failing any rule are routed for manual underwriting with a pre-populated reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Audit Log">
    Instant decisions are recorded with full rationale, bureau snapshot, and KYC status. Use the **Instant Lending Report** to monitor auto-approval volume, average time to disburse, and fallback rates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tip>

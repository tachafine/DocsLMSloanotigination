LoanOrigination.ca offers tiered subscriptions and optional modules so your organisation can align platform access with team size and functional requirements. Monthly fees are provided per client engagement, so contact your account representative for current pricing.

## Subscription tiers

| Tier | User Capacity | Target Segment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Up to 5 users | Small community lenders |
| Growth | Up to 15 users | Mid-size community lenders |
| Professional | Up to 25 users | Large lenders and credit unions |

<Note>
  Committee governance functionality is included in all tiers with no per member charge.
</Note>

## Optional modules

| Module | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Funder impact reporting | Automated collection of impact data including jobs created and maintained, demographic fields where permitted, sector, region, and investment outcomes, with scheduled quarterly and annual report generation in required funder formats. |
| AML and regulatory compliance | Risk rating of clients, ongoing monitoring rules, politically exposed person and sanctions screening, suspicious transaction escalation and reporting workflows, large cash transaction handling, record keeping, and compliance officer dashboards. |
| Bilingual interface | Complete English and French interface with user level language toggle, bilingual notification templates, bilingual document templates, Quebec specific form variants, and bilingual borrower facing materials. |
| Advanced analytics | Custom dashboard builder, portfolio performance metrics, trend analysis and forecasting, executive reporting templates, and data export for external business intelligence tools. |

Module pricing is set per client engagement. Ask your account representative which modules are recommended for your regulatory environment and reporting obligations.

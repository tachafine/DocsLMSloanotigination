---
title: "Publish a Loan Program to the Funding Directory"
sidebarTitle: "Program Listing"
description: "Publish a loan program so borrowers see it under your lender profile in the LoanOrigination.ca funding directory. Control visibility and manage borrower-facing details."
---

Loan programs configured inside your workspace can be published so they appear as listed offerings under your lender profile in the LoanOrigination.ca funding directory. This guide explains how to publish a program, what borrowers see, and how to keep internal-only products hidden from public view. For the full field reference when creating or editing a program, see [Loan Programs](/origination/loan-programs). For your overall lender profile setup, see [Directory Listing](/admin/lender-directory-listing).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configuration > Loan Programs">
    Navigate to **Configuration > Loan Programs**. Select an existing program to edit, or click **New Program** to create one from scratch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete required fields">
    Fill in the program name, purpose, amount range, interest rate, term, and any fees. These fields define the core terms borrowers evaluate when comparing products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add borrower-facing summary and required documents">
    Write a short public summary that explains who the program is for and what it covers. List any documents borrowers must submit, such as financial statements, business plans, or proof of ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Publish to Directory">
    Find the **Publish to Directory** switch near the top of the program form. Turn it on when you are ready to make the program visible to borrowers. Leave it off if the program is for internal use only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview borrower view">
    Click **Preview** to see how the program appears on your public lender page. Check that the summary, amounts, and eligibility read clearly from a borrower's perspective.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to store your changes. Published programs appear under your lender profile in the funding directory within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Unpublished programs remain available for internal origination but do not appear publicly. Use this to pilot new products or manage seasonal programs without exposing them to the directory.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Review published programs quarterly. Update rates, amounts, or eligibility to reflect current funding availability and keep borrower expectations accurate.
</Tip>

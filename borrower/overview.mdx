LoanOrigination.ca delivers the borrower experience through two aligned channels that share a single data model: a responsive web portal and a native mobile application. The mobile app is included from first release for end borrowers. Lender staff work primarily from desktop, and no staff facing mobile application is proposed.

This section covers how applicants and active borrowers interact with the platform to submit applications, upload documents, track progress, communicate with lender staff, and manage their loans.

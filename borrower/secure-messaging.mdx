LoanOrigination.ca includes secure messaging so borrowers can communicate directly with loan officers and underwriters without relying on external email. All messages are tied to the application record, creating a complete history that both you and the lender can reference.

## In-platform messaging

When a lender staff member sends you a message or requests additional information, you receive a notification alert through your chosen channels (email, SMS, or push). You can open the message thread in the web portal or mobile app and reply directly.

## Document request threads

If the lender needs a specific document, the request appears as a threaded message with a clear description of what is required. You can upload the requested file directly into the thread. The upload is categorised by document type, scanned for viruses, and transmitted with encryption, just like a standard document upload.

## Message history and attachments

Every conversation is preserved in the application record. You can scroll back through prior messages, review sent attachments, and see timestamps for each exchange. This keeps communication organised and auditable, which is especially useful if multiple staff members are involved in reviewing your application.

<Note>
  Secure messaging is available only within the LoanOrigination.ca platform. You cannot forward these messages to external email addresses, which helps protect your personal and financial information.
</Note>

## Notification alerts

You control how you are alerted about new messages through your notification preferences. Alerts can be delivered by email, SMS, or mobile push. You can adjust these settings at any time from your profile.

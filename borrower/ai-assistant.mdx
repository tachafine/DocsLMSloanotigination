LoanOrigination.ca includes a built-in AI Application Assistant to help borrowers complete applications accurately and confidently. The assistant is context aware, meaning it understands which section of the application you are working on and provides relevant guidance in plain language.

## How the assistant helps you

The AI assistant is available during the application workflow and can assist with several common tasks:

| Task | What the assistant does |
| --- | --- |
| Explain questions | Describes what a field means and why the lender is asking for it |
| Flag missing information | Identifies required fields or sections that are incomplete |
| Highlight inconsistencies | Points out answers that appear to conflict with other parts of the application |
| Guide document requirements | Tells you which supporting documents are needed based on your answers |
| Structure responses | Suggests how to organise free-text answers so they are clear and complete |

## Using the assistant

When you are unsure about a question or receive a validation warning, you can open the assistant from within the application form. The assistant reads the current field and your prior answers to give targeted guidance. It does not submit anything on your behalf; you remain in control of every answer.

<Note>
  The AI assistant does not generate financial figures on your behalf. If you need help with revenue projections, cost estimates, or other numerical inputs, the assistant will prompt you for the source data rather than inventing numbers.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If the assistant flags an inconsistency, review both related fields carefully. Correcting mismatches early reduces back-and-forth with the lender and can speed up the review process.
</Tip>

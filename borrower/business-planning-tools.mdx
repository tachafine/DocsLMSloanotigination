LoanOrigination.ca includes embedded business planning tools for small business applicants. These tools help you produce the documentation lenders need without starting from a blank page. Outputs are formatted for underwriting review and can be exported for your own records.

## Business plan generator

The business plan builder guides you through a series of structured inputs covering the core components lenders expect to see. You do not need prior business planning experience to complete it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter business details">
    Provide your industry classification, revenue model, cost structure, staffing plans, and intended use of funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review templated sections">
    The system generates standard sections including executive summary, market overview, operations plan, and financial assumptions based on your inputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export your plan">
    Download the completed business plan as a PDF or DOCX file. The platform keeps a version history so you can track changes over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cash flow templates

The platform provides structured cash flow and financial projection templates. These templates are designed to feed directly into the underwriting file rather than being submitted as detached spreadsheets.

| Template purpose | What it captures |
| --- | --- |
| Cash flow projections | Monthly inflows, outflows, and net position |
| Financial assumptions | Revenue growth, expense trends, and capital needs |
| Use of funds | How loan proceeds will be allocated |

<Note>
  Cash flow templates are configured by your lender to match their underwriting requirements. The exact fields and time periods available depend on the loan program you are applying for.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Save your progress regularly. The business plan generator supports autosave, and you can return to edit sections before final submission.
</Tip>

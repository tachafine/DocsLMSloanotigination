The Community Futures section of the directory lists Community Futures Development Corporations (CFDCs) operating across rural and northern Canada. CFDCs are non-profit, community-based organizations that provide small business loans, business advisory services, and community economic development support in their designated service regions.

## What Community Futures offer

CFDCs typically provide the following services to entrepreneurs and small businesses in their region:

- Small business loans, often up to $150,000, with flexible terms for start-ups and existing businesses
- Business planning and coaching for new and expanding businesses
- Community economic development projects and partnerships
- Sector-specific programs, including youth, women in business, and Indigenous entrepreneurship

Exact program details, loan amounts, and eligibility vary by CFDC.

## Coverage

The directory maps 267 Community Futures organizations. Each region of Canada is served by a designated CFDC, so borrowers should search by their location to find the organization that serves their community.

## Search and filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the directory">
    Navigate to **Directory > Community Futures**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by province">
    Select your province or territory to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by service area">
    Enter your municipality or postal code to find the CFDC that covers your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the profile">
    Open a listing to see contact details, funding limits, eligibility, and named programs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

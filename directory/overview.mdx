The LoanOrigination.ca directory is a public listing of community-focused lenders operating across Canada. It maps 267 Community Futures organizations, more than 400 credit unions, and more than 50 Indigenous Financial Institutions, giving borrowers and referral partners a single place to find the right funding source by region, lender type, and program focus.

## Who uses the directory

The directory is used by three primary audiences:

- **Borrowers and entrepreneurs** searching for a lender that matches their region, sector, and funding needs.
- **Referral partners** including banks, municipalities, incubators, and accountants that need to route clients to the right community lender.
- **Lenders** who want visibility to prospective borrowers and referral partners across Canada.

## Structured lender profiles

Each directory listing provides consistent, structured information so borrowers can compare lenders side by side:

| Profile field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Lender type | Community Futures, credit union, Indigenous Financial Institution, or other community lender |
| Region served | Province, territory, and specific service area or catchment |
| Funding limits | Typical loan amount ranges and program ceilings |
| Eligibility | Who can apply, including sector, stage, and demographic focus where applicable |
| Programs offered | Named loan programs, grants, and advisory services |
| Contact information | Website, phone, email, and physical address |

<Tip>
  Borrowers can filter listings by province, lender type, and funding range. Referral partners can bookmark filtered views for their most common client profiles.
</Tip>

The Indigenous Financial Institutions (IFI) section of the directory lists autonomous, Indigenous-controlled, community-based financial organizations serving First Nations, Metis, and Inuit entrepreneurs across every province and territory in Canada.

## What IFIs offer

Indigenous Financial Institutions provide developmental lending and business support tailored to Indigenous entrepreneurs and communities, including:

- Developmental business loans for start-up, expansion, and acquisition
- Business support services, including planning, coaching, and aftercare
- Sector-specific and community-specific programs
- Access to broader Indigenous economic development initiatives

Programs and eligibility criteria vary by IFI and are set by each community-based organization.

## Coverage

The directory includes more than 50 Indigenous Financial Institutions with service areas spanning First Nations reserves, Metis communities, Inuit regions, and urban Indigenous populations across Canada.

## Search and filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the directory">
    Navigate to **Directory > Indigenous Financial Institutions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by province or territory">
    Select a region to see IFIs with service areas that cover your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by community focus">
    Narrow by First Nations, Metis, Inuit, or urban Indigenous focus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a listing">
    Review contact details, funding limits, and available programs and support services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  IFIs operate independently. Loan decisions, eligibility, and program terms are set by each institution based on its mandate and community priorities.
</Note>

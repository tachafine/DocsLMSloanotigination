The credit union section of the directory lists member-owned cooperative financial institutions serving communities across Canada. Credit unions provide personal, commercial, and agricultural lending, often with a stronger local focus than national banks.

## What credit unions offer

Credit unions in the directory typically provide:

- Personal loans and lines of credit
- Small business and commercial lending
- Agricultural and rural business financing
- Community-focused programs tied to their membership base

Programs, eligibility, and funding limits vary by institution and province.

## Coverage

The directory includes more than 400 credit unions across Canada. Coverage spans provincially regulated credit unions, federally regulated cooperative banks, and caisses populaires in Quebec.

## Search and filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the directory">
    Navigate to **Directory > Credit Unions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by province">
    Choose a province or select all to browse nationally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by lending focus">
    Narrow by personal, commercial, agricultural, or Indigenous-focused lending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a listing">
    Review contact information, service area, and available programs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

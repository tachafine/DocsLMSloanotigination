Every listing in the LoanOrigination.ca directory follows a consistent structure so borrowers and referral partners can compare lenders side by side. This page explains what appears in each profile, how listings are sourced, and how lenders can update their information.

## Profile structure

Each lender profile contains the following sections:

| Section | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Lender name, logo where available, lender type, and primary service region |
| About | Short description of the lender's mission, mandate, and history |
| Funding | Loan amount ranges, typical term lengths, and named programs |
| Eligibility | Who can apply, including geography, sector, stage, and demographic focus where applicable |
| Contact | Website, phone, email, and physical address |
| Additional resources | Links to application forms, program documentation, and partner organizations |

## How listings are sourced

Listings are compiled from publicly available sources, including national association directories and each lender's own website. The directory reflects the Canadian community-lending ecosystem and is maintained as new organizations are identified or existing organizations update their programs.

## Claim or update a listing

Lenders can request updates to their profile to keep funding limits, eligibility, and contact information accurate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your listing">
    Search the directory for your organization and open the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit an update request">
    Use the **Request update** link on the profile to send corrected information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    A member of the LoanOrigination.ca team will verify that the request comes from an authorized representative of the lender.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the update">
    You will receive a preview of the updated profile for confirmation before it is published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Directory listings are provided for informational purposes. LoanOrigination.ca does not underwrite, endorse, or guarantee the programs offered by listed lenders. Each borrower is responsible for confirming current terms and eligibility directly with the lender.
</Note>

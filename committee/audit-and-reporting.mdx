LoanOrigination.ca captures every committee action in a permanent, tamper-evident audit trail. Whether you need to prove quorum for a funder, satisfy an external auditor, or reconstruct the history behind a loan decision, the system preserves who reviewed what, when they acted, and the outcome of every vote.

## What the audit trail captures

Each committee session generates a detailed log that includes:

- **Reviewer identity**: the name and role of every participant who opened or acted on the application
- **Timestamps**: exact date and time for each view, comment, vote, and status change
- **Vote records**: how each member voted, including abstentions and the rationale entered
- **Comments and annotations**: full text of every comment made during deliberation
- **Document access**: which files each reviewer opened or downloaded
- **Conflict-of-interest declarations**: any disclosures filed before or during the session

<Note>
  All timestamps are recorded in the tenant's configured time zone and stored with Canadian data residency. Logs cannot be edited or deleted by committee members or administrators.
</Note>

## Exporting decision registers

You can export a complete decision register for any committee session or date range. The export includes a chronological list of motions, votes, and outcomes formatted for auditor review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the committee session">
    Navigate to **Committee** > **Sessions** and select the session you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the register">
    Click **Export** > **Decision Register**. Choose PDF or CSV format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and distribute">
    Save the file locally or send it directly to an auditor or funder contact from the share dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quorum and conflict-of-interest reports

For governance and compliance, the system can produce two specialized reports:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Quorum Record" icon="users">
    Lists attendees, their roles, and whether quorum was achieved at the start of each vote. Use this to satisfy funder covenants or board governance requirements.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Conflict-of-Interest Declaration" icon="shield-check">
    Compiles all disclosures filed by committee members for a given period, including the nature of the conflict and whether the member recused themselves from voting.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Accessing reports

Committee chairs and designated compliance officers can access all reports from **Committee** > **Audit and Reporting**. Members with read-only audit roles can view logs but cannot export or share them. If you need additional permissions, contact your tenant administrator.

<Tip>
  Schedule automated exports of decision registers to your document management system by setting up a recurring export rule in **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Scheduled Exports**.
</Tip>

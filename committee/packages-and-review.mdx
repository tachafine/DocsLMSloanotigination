---
title: "Assemble and Distribute Secure Committee Loan Packages"
sidebarTitle: "Packages & Review"
description: "Officers assemble loan application packages and publish them securely to committee members with permission scoping, time-bound access, and inline commenting."
---

Before a committee votes, the lending officer must assemble a complete application package and distribute it securely. LoanOrigination.ca lets you pull documents from the loan file, arrange them in a review sequence, and publish to assigned members with strict permission controls so reviewers see only what they need, only when they are meant to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assemble the package">
    Open the loan file and go to **Committee > Prepare Package**. Select the documents to include: the application form, credit memo, bureau report, financial statements, collateral appraisals, and any supporting documents. Arrange them in the order members should review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the review window">
    Choose the start and end dates for the review period. Members cannot access the package before the window opens, and access is revoked automatically when it closes. This ensures all members review on the same timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign members and scope permissions">
    Select the committee members who will review the package. By default, each member sees the full package. To restrict visibility, click **Scope Permissions** and hide specific documents from individual members. For example, exclude the officer credit memo from members who will vote independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the package">
    Click **Publish to Committee**. Members receive a notification with a secure link. The package opens in a view-only viewer inside LoanOrigination.ca; documents cannot be downloaded or printed unless your tenant policy explicitly allows it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and comment">
    Committee members open the package from their dashboard. They can scroll through documents, highlight passages, and add comments or recommendations inline. Comments are threaded by document and visible to other reviewers in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track review progress">
    Officers can monitor who has opened the package, which documents they viewed, and what comments they left. Use this view to follow up with members who have not yet reviewed before the window closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the same package for both pre-meeting review and the live meeting. Members who reviewed early will already be familiar with the file when the agenda is called.
</Tip>

<Note>
  All package views, comments, and downloads are logged to the audit trail. Members cannot delete their own comments after posting.
</Note>

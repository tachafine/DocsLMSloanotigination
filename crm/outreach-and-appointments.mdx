Community lending is built on relationships. LoanOrigination.ca helps you stay close to every borrower and prospect by logging every call, email, and meeting in one place. You can schedule appointments, send bilingual messages from templates, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks with follow-up tasks and reminders.

## Log outreach activity

Every interaction with a contact or loan file can be recorded so your team has full context. Open any contact or file, then click **Log Activity** to capture the details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the activity type">
    Select **Call**, **Email**, **Meeting**, **Note**, or **Task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details">
    Record the date, duration, subject, and a summary of what was discussed. Link the activity to one or more loan files if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose whether the activity is visible to the whole team or restricted to specific roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The activity appears on the contact timeline and any linked files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule appointments with calendar sync

Book appointments directly from a contact profile or loan file. LoanOrigination.ca syncs with your Google or Outlook calendar so invites are sent automatically and availability is checked in real time.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Send invites" icon="calendar">
    Pick a time slot, add participants, and send calendar invites without leaving the platform.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Block time" icon="clock">
    Appointments appear on your synced calendar with contact details and file links included.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Send bilingual email and SMS

Reach contacts in their preferred language using templates. LoanOrigination.ca supports English and French templates out of the box, and you can create custom templates for each language.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Choose from appointment reminders, document requests, welcome messages, or custom templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview in the contact's language">
    The platform loads the French version automatically if the contact's language preference is set to French.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send via email or SMS">
    Pick the channel, review the preview, and send immediately or schedule for later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set follow-up tasks and reminders

After any interaction, create a follow-up task so the next step is clear. Assign it to yourself or a teammate, set a due date, and add a reminder. Tasks appear on your dashboard and in daily reminder digests.

<Tip>
  Set a follow-up task before you close an activity log. This keeps momentum alive and prevents prospects from going cold.
</Tip>

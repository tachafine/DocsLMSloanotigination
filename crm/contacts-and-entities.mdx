LoanOrigination.ca keeps every relationship in one place with unified contact records. Whether you are working with an individual borrower, a corporation, a guarantor, or a referral partner, each contact lives in a single profile that your whole team can access and update.

## Contact types

When you create a contact, choose the type that matches the relationship. Each type unlocks relevant fields and workflows.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Individual" icon="user">
    Borrowers, co-applicants, and guarantors. Capture name, address, employment, and bilingual preference.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Business" icon="building">
    Corporations, cooperatives, and nonprofits. Record legal name, business number, and ownership structure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Referral Partner" icon="handshake">
    Community organizations, brokers, and event sources. Track referral volume and funded attribution.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Guarantor" icon="shield">
    Personal or corporate guarantors linked to specific loan files.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Link contacts to entities

Community lending often involves complex relationships. A business owner may guarantee a corporate loan, or a referral partner may also be a past borrower. Use entity links to map these connections without creating duplicate records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a contact profile">
    Navigate to the contact you want to link from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a related entity">
    Click **Related Entities**, then **Link Contact**. Search for the target contact or business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the relationship type">
    Choose a label such as _Owner of_, _Guarantor for_, _Referred by_, or create a custom label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the link">
    The relationship now appears on both profiles and updates automatically if details change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Attach contacts to loan files

Every loan file lists the contacts involved. Add borrowers, guarantors, and business entities so underwriters see the full picture. Changes to a contact profile push to all linked files, keeping data consistent.

## Bilingual preferences and custom fields

Canada's community lenders serve diverse populations. Record each contact's preferred language (English or French) so outreach, documents, and portal messages appear in the right language. Add custom fields per contact type to track what matters to your organization, such as community membership, Indigenous status, or program eligibility.

<Tip>
  Set a contact's language preference before sending your first email or SMS. Templates automatically switch to the preferred language if a translation exists.
</Tip>

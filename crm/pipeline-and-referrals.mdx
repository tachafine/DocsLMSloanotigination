LoanOrigination.ca gives your lending team a clear view of every prospect as they move from first inquiry to funded loan. The pipeline shows where each opportunity stands, while referral tracking connects funded loans back to the people and programs that brought them in.

## Pipeline stages

Every prospect record moves through a standard set of stages. Your team can see at a glance which files need attention and where bottlenecks form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New lead">
    A prospect enters the system from a walk-in, web form, referral, or event. Capture contact details and initial need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualification">
    A lending officer reviews eligibility, pre-screens credit, and confirms the prospect fits your program criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application">
    The prospect submits a full application. Required documents are requested and tracked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Underwriting">
    The file moves to assessment. Risk, cash flow, and collateral are evaluated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    The loan is approved with terms. The prospect reviews and accepts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funding">
    Documents are signed and funds are disbursed. The prospect becomes a borrower.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log and track lead sources

Knowing where prospects come from helps you invest in the right outreach. When you create a prospect, select a source from the dropdown or add a custom source.

| Source type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Walk-in | Branch visit, community drop-in |
| Referral partner | Nonprofit, broker, accountant |
| Web | Online application, website chat |
| Event | Trade show, community fair, workshop |
| Other | Phone call, social media, direct mail |

## Attribute funded loans to referral partners

When a referred prospect reaches the Funding stage, LoanOrigination.ca automatically links the funded loan to the original referral partner. You can run reports by partner, by source type, or by date range to see which channels drive the most volume.

## Referral partner scorecards

Review partner performance from the **Referral Partners** view. Each partner card shows:

- Total leads submitted
- Conversion rate to application
- Conversion rate to funded loan
- Average time from lead to funding
- Dollar value of loans funded

<Note>
  Scorecard data updates nightly. For real-time numbers during a partner review, click **Refresh** on the partner profile.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the date filter on scorecards to compare partner performance quarter over quarter and identify trends early.
</Tip>

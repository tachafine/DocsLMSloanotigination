After a loan is funded, your relationship with the borrower continues. LoanOrigination.ca helps you manage renewals, top-up requests, and post-funding support cases from a single workspace. Every case carries a clear owner, status history, and resolution record so your team and auditors can see exactly what happened and when.

## Renewal and top-up tracking

Renewals and top-ups appear as structured records linked to the original loan file. The system calculates eligibility based on program rules, payment history, and remaining term. You can initiate a renewal directly from the borrower's profile or let the system prompt you when conditions are met.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review renewal eligibility">
    Open the borrower's profile and select the **Renewals** tab. The eligibility summary shows qualifying criteria and any outstanding conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate or schedule the renewal">
    Click **Start Renewal** to begin a new application, or click **Schedule Reminder** to notify the borrower and your team at a future date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the renewal through to decision">
    The renewal application follows the same workflow as an original loan, with full committee review and audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Automated renewal reminders fire on the schedule you configure per program. You can set lead time, recipient list, and bilingual message templates in **Settings** > **Communications** > **Renewal Reminders**.
</Note>

## Post-funding cases

Cases capture any post-funding interaction that is not a standard payment or renewal. Common case types include:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Guidance Requests" icon="message-circle-question">
    Borrowers ask for business advice, program clarification, or documentation support. Assign to a relationship manager and track through to resolution.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Business Coaching" icon="presentation">
    Scheduled or ad-hoc coaching sessions. Record session notes, action items, and follow-up dates in the case file.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Complaint Handling" icon="alert-triangle">
    Formal complaints are escalated automatically based on severity. The case owner documents intake, investigation, response, and closure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="General Inquiries" icon="mail">
    Any other borrower communication that needs tracking, assignment, or follow-up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Case management workflow

Every case moves through a standard lifecycle: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Pending Borrower**, **Resolved**, and **Closed**. At each transition, the system records the owner, timestamp, and any resolution notes. You can reopen a closed case if new information arises, and the full history remains visible.

<Tip>
  Use case categories and tags to build dashboards that show workload by team member, case type, or average time to resolution. Find these in **CRM** > **Cases** > **Dashboard**.
</Tip>
